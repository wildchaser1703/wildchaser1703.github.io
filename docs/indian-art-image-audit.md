# Indian Art Atlas image audit

Checked 25 November 2026 against main at `d1f75bb57659aa790dcbc64a6bb95dbc27f9f386`. The baseline held 56 work and research records, including 10 illustrated works, with 39 traditions, 24 makers and 19 published articles. All baseline work routes are retained.

The [structured audit](../data/atlas-image-audit.json) links every entry to its object source and records the photographer or image provider, licence, credit and adaptation evidence when known. The audit distinguishes an exact reproduction from a group, installation or process photograph. The latter can document a record when clearly labelled. Exact-title, maker and accession searches supplemented source pages. Failure to locate an independently cleared photograph is time-bound.

This release publishes 27 illustrated records. Ten are reverified baseline images, one illustrates a previously unillustrated baseline record and sixteen are distinct additions. Forty-five original records still lack displayable imagery.

The original remote URLs were replaced with accession-checked local reproductions where verified. The Puri pilgrimage painting already appeared as regional context and now has a catalogue entry. Goa’s Kaavi record now shows a photograph documenting the historic wall decoration in place. The other additions have their own catalogue identities. The Manipur Longpi pots and Bidri vase context photographs were taken out of the templates pending confirmation of rights in the depicted objects.
